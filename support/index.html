App Support & In-App Purchase Assistance

Published to meet Apple App Store submission requirements (Support URL) and Google Play developer requirements.

How to Restore Purchases on iOS / Android:

  1. Ensure you are signed into the exact Apple ID or Google Account used for the original transaction.
  2. Open the Monik Studio game/app, navigate to Settings > tap "Restore Purchases".
  3. If items do not unlock immediately, please allow up to 5-10 minutes for store receipt synchronization.
  4. Need further help? Write directly to contact@monikstudio.com with your Order Receipt ID (GPA.XXXX or Apple invoice).

Common Troubleshooting Questions

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Log in with the same Apple ID or Google Account on your new device. In the game/app settings, tap "Restore Purchases". Apple App Store and Google Play will validate your receipt tokens and restore all non-consumable unlocks for free.

Can I play Monik Studio games offline?

Yes! All our games are built offline-first. Leaderboards and cloud saves will automatically synchronize the next time your device connects to the internet.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Email us directly at contact@monikstudio.com. Please mention your device model, operating system version (e.g. iOS 18 or Android 14), and app version.

How do I request a refund?

All transactions are handled directly by Apple or Google. On iOS, visit reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, visit play.google.com/store/account/orderhistory.

Need Direct Developer Assistance?

Please email our team with your device model, app title, and store receipt.
